Glossa

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Glossa shows YouTube subtitles in two languages at once in Safari, and helps you learn words from them. It is built to work without collecting anything about you. In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your settings and saved words stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Settings — your translation language, text size, colors and other preferences.
  • Saved words — words you save, with the subtitle line they came from and a link to that moment in the video.
  • Daily usage — which videos you watched today, used only to count the free daily limit. It resets every day.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and shared only between the Glossa app and its Safari extension. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

How subtitles and translations work

  • The Safari extension runs only on youtube.com. It reads the subtitles that YouTube already provides for the video you are watching, including YouTube's own translation into your chosen language, and draws them over the video. These requests go directly from your browser to YouTube, as they would without Glossa. YouTube's handling of data is described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Word translations and Japanese, Chinese and Korean pronunciation are produced on your device by Apple's built-in translation and language features. Languages you download for this are managed by iOS and shared with other apps.
  • The extension does not read other websites, your browsing history, passwords or form entries.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Data we collect

None. Glossa has no servers, uses no analytics or advertising tools, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Glossa is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If Glossa ever starts processing personal data, we will explain it here before it happens.
