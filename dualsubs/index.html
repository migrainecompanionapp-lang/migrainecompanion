DualSubs Support

Dual-language YouTube subtitles in Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Turn on DualSubs in Safari

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Tap DualSubs and turn on Allow Extension.
  3. Under Permissions, set youtube.com to Allow.
  4. Open any YouTube video in Safari. Subtitles turn on by themselves.

Questions

The subtitles don't appear.

Check that the extension is allowed for youtube.com (see above). If a video has no subtitles at all, DualSubs shows "No subtitles on this video". Otherwise, tap CC in the player once.

How do I translate a single word?

Tap the word in the subtitles. The first time, open DualSubs and download your languages under Word Translation, or in Settings → Apps → Translate. Translations then work offline. Word translation needs iOS 26.4 or later.

How do I replay a line?

Tap the translated line. To stop after every line, turn on Pause After Each Line in the app.

Can I watch full screen?

Turn your iPhone sideways and the video fills the screen with tappable subtitles. YouTube's own full-screen button also shows both lines, in the system's subtitle style.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DualSubs. Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchases in the app.
